## 📌 Questionnaire & Live Google Sheets Integration

This repository contains the standalone clean questionnaire website designed to collect responses for the academic study:
> **"From Cart to Conversion: An Integrated Orange, JASP and SmartPLS-SEM Approach to Understanding and Reducing E-Commerce Cart Abandonment"**

All responses submitted by respondents are automatically stored in your **Google Sheet** owned by `evarun5115@gmail.com`. Whenever you want, you can simply open the Google Sheet in Google Drive and download it as **CSV** or **Excel** to run your analysis in **SmartPLS**, **JASP**, or **Orange**.

---

## 🟢 1. Google Sheets Setup (One-Time Setup)

1. Open your browser and go to **[script.google.com](https://script.google.com/)** (signed into `evarun5115@gmail.com`).
2. Click **+ New Project**.
3. Copy the code from [`create_google_form.gs`](create_google_form.gs) and paste it into the editor.
4. Click **Run ▶** at the top.
5. The script automatically creates:
   - **Official Google Form**
   - **Linked Google Sheet** in your Google Drive titled *"Cart Abandonment Survey Responses (Live Data Sheet)"*.
6. Open your Google Drive anytime to view responses populating row-by-row in real time!

---

## 📥 2. How to Download Your Data for Analysis

1. Open your Google Drive at [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com/).
2. Open **"Cart Abandonment Survey Responses (Live Data Sheet)"**.
3. Click **File** > **Download** > **Comma-separated values (.csv)** or **Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)**.
4. Import directly into **SmartPLS 4**, **JASP**, or **Orange Data Mining** for SEM model assessment, reliability tests, and machine learning models.
